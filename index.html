FireAwayCapstone project

Winner of the EPI-USE Labs Most Innovative Final Year Project Award

  • Architecture & delivery. Engineered FireAway, an AI-driven wildfire spread modelling and decision-support platform for EPI-USE Africa, serving high-risk communities and first responders across South Africa. Designed a layered monolith with an asynchronous, queue-based distributed GPU tier (cache-aside, claim-check) for deep-learning forecasting and cellular-automaton simulation, deployed in the cloud with blue/green CI/CD.

  • Cloud infrastructure & DevOps. Containerised the end-to-end stack with Docker and architected a production-ready AWS environment using EC2, ECR, RDS, S3, SQS, SES and IAM for secure, distributed real-time processing and alerting.
  • Agile team collaboration. Co-developed the platform within an agile team of five engineers, driving client milestone deliveries, technical documentation and cross-functional integration.
